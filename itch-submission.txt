Description（可直接粘贴）
Two worlds cannot coexist. Your past self can.

SHIFT is a browser platformer about two overlapping realities: amber SOL and moonlit LUN.
A bridge in one world is a ghost in the other. A harmless outline can become a deadly spike.
Read both layers, jump, then SHIFT at just the right moment.

Across nine handcrafted campaign levels, collect phase shards, dodge world-bound patrols, and aim for
three stars: finish, collect every shard, and survive without a death.

Level 5, ONE MORE JUMP, unlocks double-jumping: release and press jump again in the air.
You get one extra boost until landing; switching worlds does not recharge it.

The finale, HOLD THIS FOR ME, changes the rules. SHIFT leaves a three-second double of your old self.
Leave it holding a pressure plate in SOL, race to its partner in LUN, and unlock a resonance gate
together. There can only be one echo: each new shift replaces the last. Pause freezes its timer.

Features:
- Nine levels, checkpoints, local progress and best times.
- Radial world transitions, a trailing scarf, embers, spores and moonlit meteors.
- Procedural WebAudio music that changes with the world; no downloaded audio assets.
- Optional Assist mode: slower patrols and more forgiving spike collisions.
- Keyboard controls and touch buttons. Desktop keyboard / mobile landscape recommended.
- No install, no account required to play, and no external runtime dependencies.

Browser interface and tutorials: Simplified Chinese.
Both the browser build and the native Windows edition include WAIT FOR ME, a separate ten-chapter
station journey with its own map and progress. Instead of standing still, your double replays your
last three seconds of movement in the old world. Record plates in order, leave your past self upstairs,
take a right-to-left route, and catch the last train. Later stops replay jumps, send you back for your
partner, link three doors to one recording, and ask you to SHIFT between stepping stones in midair
before arriving home at sunrise. Every chapter has three shards, checkpoints and a single jump.
All six earlier stops and their saved records are retained.
The native Windows edition adds Chinese/English menus, gamepad controls and native Godot rendering.

Controls:
Arrow keys / A D — move
Space / W / Up — jump; release early for a shorter jump
From level 5: release and press again in midair to double-jump (touch: release and tap ▲ again)
Shift — switch worlds (and leave an echo in the finale)
R — restart level
Esc — pause; Assist mode is in this menu
M — mute

上传与提交步骤
1. 用 package.ps1 生成 HTML5 ZIP。选择文件名以 SHIFT-Phase-Walker-HTML5 开头的最新 ZIP。
2. 在自己的 itch.io 账号中新建项目，项目类型选 HTML。
3. 上传 ZIP，勾选类似“This file will be played in the browser”的浏览器游玩选项。
4. 嵌入区域建议 960 × 640；启用全屏选项。点击游戏区域后键盘输入才会聚焦。
5. 用 cover.png 作封面，从 screenshots/ 或 echo.png 选截图，粘贴简介与操作说明，价格设为免费。
6. 在实际 itch.io 嵌入页试玩：开始、跳跃、切换、暂停、静音、重开与存档。
   存档保存在当前浏览器/站点，file://、本地预览与 itch.io 的存档互不共享。
7. 补上实际 GitHub 公共仓库地址，确认页面可公开访问，再发布作品页面。
8. 进入 Game Off 2026 比赛页面，选择提交作品，选中刚发布的游戏并填写表单。
9. 检查作品确实出现在比赛提交列表中，保存确认信息。发布作品不等于提交比赛。
10. 若之后更新 ZIP，重新验证线上试玩；不要只更新本地文件。
